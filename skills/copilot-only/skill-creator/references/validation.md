# Validation Guidance

## What to validate

- Does the frontmatter clearly describe what the skill does?
- Does the trigger wording make activation likely in the right situations?
- Is the body concise enough?
- Are the critical rules easy to spot?
- Are references/scripts/assets actually justified?

## Independent validation

If you can use another agent or reviewer:

- give it the task, not the intended answer
- ask whether the skill triggers correctly
- inspect the output for ambiguity or missing guidance

## Warning signs

- the skill repeats documentation instead of referencing it
- the body is much longer than the workflow really requires
- the trigger wording is too vague
- the skill includes many sections that are never operationally useful
